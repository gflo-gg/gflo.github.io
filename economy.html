CORES

Cores are the currency of this system. You earn them by being here — watching, chatting, subscribing, raiding — on Twitch and YouTube alike. One viewer, one balance.

Spend them on games in chat, influence over the stream, and — in a future patch — ripping virtual card packs. Cores have no cash value, cannot be bought, and cannot be sold. The only way to get them is to run the process. See RULES.TXT for the fine print.
